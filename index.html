[ 03 / process ]

How contract work runs

The same pipeline we use on our own show, pointed at your project.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell us the idea, the audience, and the deadline. We come back with scope and a quote.

  2. 02

    Boards

    Concept, look, and a shot plan, so you see the piece before we build it.

  3. 03

    Build

    AI production, animation, or a shoot. Usually a mix, with check-ins along the way.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final files in the formats you need, cut for YouTube, social, or broadcast.
